- `POST /storefront/wishlist/items` adds a product. A second add of the same product returns 200 and does not insert a duplicate. The `wishlist_item` table enforces that with a unique `(customer_id, product_id)` constraint.

- `GET /storefront/wishlist/items/{wishlistItemId}` returns the item id, customer id, product id, and availability for the cart service.
- Guests get 401. The filter chain requires authentication on `/storefront/wishlist/**`.
